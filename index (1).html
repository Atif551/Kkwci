Atif Automora
script → footage → voice → captions → export
1
API keys
Saved locally in this browser only. Never uploaded anywhere. Rest of the tool unlocks once these are verified.
Enter your Pexels and Gemini keys, then tap Save & connect. ElevenLabs is optional — add it only if you want ElevenLabs voices.
2
Script & format
One line = one scene = one clip. Next line, next clip. Pick the orientation before fetching footage, so clips match from the start.
3
Scenes & footage
Each scene gets an AI keyword and a matching Pexels clip. Edit any keyword and re-fetch if needed.
4
Voice over
AI voice (Gemini or ElevenLabs), your own recording, or a custom spoken script per scene.
It gets split across all scenes automatically, proportional to how much text each line has — you don't need to record or upload per line.
✓Voice over ready for all scenes.
5
Caption style
Word-grouped animated captions, burned into the export. Sized to fit, not full-screen.

Format & transitions
7
Export
Renders fully in your browser with ffmpeg.wasm — nothing uploaded anywhere.
Browser export is CPU/RAM bound. 720p and videos under ~2 minutes render comfortably on most phones and laptops. Keep this tab in the foreground while it renders.
